# Wiki screenshot checklist

Capture each item from the intended build and record its actual four-part application version. A checked item means a capture exists, not that its article is published or fully verified. Current captures are from 0.2.29.0. Use a demonstration project; keep account details, credentials and unrelated windows outside the image.

Save a full-window original and record article IDs, version, state and caption in the capture manifest. Add focused crops or annotations later when they improve readability. Before/after comparisons must use matching framing and change one relevant parameter at a time.

Each layer/effect entry calls for its controls and a rendered result; dense panels may need more than one frame. Do not force imported/special layer kinds into an Add layer menu where they do not appear. Screenshot coverage is separate from behavior verification.
