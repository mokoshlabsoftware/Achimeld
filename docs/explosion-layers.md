# Explosion layers

Add **Explosion** in the Icon layers picker. Its icon is a nine-arm starburst.

**Base brightness** is a fixed top-level slider from 0–200%. **Shape > Brightness variation** independently samples a 0–200% multiplier for each object. Base brightness multiplies that sample and the gradient RGB, preserving alpha. Its default is 100%, with variation fixed at 100%. Brightness draws follow all geometry and position draws, preserving existing seeded shapes. The action bar's **Invert colors** toggle reverses gradient RGB before brightness without moving gradient stops or changing transparency; it reuses the generated half-light/half-dark icon.

**Construction** displays actual SDF coverage, brightness variation, softness and pre-polar blur from the renderer, replacing solid polygon fills. SDF gradients appear in the construction image; spatial gradients remain a post-projection overlay. A bounded host worker publishes a quick strip before the refined strip, cancels obsolete generations, and retains the last successful GPU texture while updating. Preview errors appear below the Layers controls so their arrival does not shift sliders.

The compact action bar keeps **Mask**, **Presets**, **Count range**, **Randomize**, **Invert colors**, **Different seed for each achievement**, **Cover canvas**, and **Bottom scaling curve** together as icon-only buttons on one row. Hover a button for its label; selected fills show enabled toggles and an assigned mask. The curve button shows the active curve's icon and opens a picker with icons and labels. The Mask icon retains the create/use-existing/remove picker and the mask's preview, channel and inversion controls. **Overall scale** keeps its label, single-value knob and percentage on one horizontal row.

Directly below Overall scale, **Shape X** and **Shape Y** are fixed sliders from **1–10×**. They multiply the width and height after each object's random dimensions and scale have been sampled, without changing placement or the random sequence.

**Fill power** progressively multiplies each object's horizontal width toward the bottom of the construction rectangle, filling the center after polar projection. It runs from 0–900% extra width (1–10× at the bottom). **Fill stretch** controls how much of the rectangle is affected, measured upward from the bottom: 50% starts at the midpoint, 100% starts at the top, and 0% disables widening. The icon dropdown on the action bar chooses **Linear**, **Quadratic** (growth concentrated near the bottom), or **Logarithmic** (earlier, fuller growth). All curves preserve width at the start and reach the same bottom multiplier. Widening is centered on each object's own position and wraps at the strip seam; vertical dimensions, positions, random samples, and the portion above the start stay unchanged. It applies before construction blur, to both deltoids/ellipses and legacy triangles. SDF colors follow the deformed shape's original normalized interior. Power defaults to zero, stretch to 50%, and the curve to Linear, preserving old appearances. The fields `bottomPower`, `bottomStretch`, and `bottomCurve` persist with recipes, history, projects, and preset collections.

The **Shape** foldout starts with a fixed **Deltoid → Ellipse** slider. At 0% the shape is a kite symmetric from left to right, with a shared width and waist. Increasing the slider rounds its sides continuously; at 100% it is an ellipse whose bounds retain the sampled top and bottom tips. **Top height** and **Bottom height** are independent range sliders measuring each vertex's distance from the waist. Drag either round knob to adjust that end, or drag the blue bar to move both ends together while preserving their separation. The bar highlights on hover and stops at the slider limits; knobs retain their normal color when selected. Arrow keys adjust the focused knob or bar, with Shift for larger steps. Top maps outward in the final explosion; bottom maps inward. With the morph at 0%, either height can reach zero to flatten that end into a triangle; both heights zero produce an empty shape at every morph value.

The foldout also provides **Width**, **Offset**, **Sharpness**, **Random scale** ranges and a single **Global scale** slider. Every object independently samples its dimensions and random scale. Global scale × sampled random scale multiplies its shared width and both vertex heights together, preserving its waist and symmetry. Scale controls run from 1–200%. Width, heights and offset use the rectangle's height as their unit. Offset ranges from **−150% to +150%**; positive offset moves the waist toward the bottom/inward side. Sharpness runs from soft (0) to crisp (1), retaining pixel antialiasing.

Phase 1 places these shapes along a periodic rectangle, with a sampled aspect ratio between **1:1 and 10:1**. Each shape has one continuous distance field, with no internal edge at the waist. Phase 2 maps the rectangle's horizontal direction around a full circle. The top edge maps to the outer radius and the bottom edge to the center; the waist starts halfway along the radius. Sampling wraps across the rectangle seam. New layers enable **Cover canvas** and start **Overall scale** at **100%**: the polar radius is half the canvas diagonal, enclosing all four icon corners on square or rectangular canvases. Overall scale is a single-value knob from 1–200%; per-object variation remains under Shape > Random scale. Editing the knob fixes both stored overall scale bounds to the chosen value. Existing ranges retain their current sampled appearance until edited. Turning Cover canvas off uses the shorter half-edge. Existing recipes without `coverCanvas` retain their previous radius and stored scale.

The fixed **Radial blur** and **Angular blur** sliders run from 0–100%. They soften the construction rectangle before polar projection: radial blur spreads vertically, toward and away from the center; angular blur spreads horizontally, around the circle. Angular sampling wraps across the seam. Blur is independent of the random seed. At zero, rendering retains the original direct sampling path. With blur enabled, a bounded construction texture (at most 262,144 pixels) is softened using three separable box passes. SDF colors blur with their shape coverage; Radial and Linear gradient overlays keep their final-canvas coordinates.

Gradient modes are **Radial**, **Linear** and **SDF**. The first two use final canvas coordinates. SDF uses signed distance inside the shape before polar projection: stop 0 colors the boundary and stop 1 the deepest normalized interior. Straight deltoids use exact edge distances; rounded shapes use a gradient-corrected superellipse distance approximation. The field follows width, vertex heights and scale independently of edge sharpness. Stop transparency multiplies the shape coverage. The shared **Reverse gradient** icon mirrors all stops, including their transparency.

**Objects** is an exact integer count by default, from **1 to 512**. The **Count range** icon switches it to an inclusive integer range. **Regularity** is a single slider: **0** gives exactly equal spacing; **1** gives independent uniform random positions. Intermediate values increase positional jitter. **Randomize** changes the stored seed; **Different seed for each achievement** optionally varies a shared recipe by achievement identity. All samples are deterministic, with no animation or clock input.

The shared Transform row has single-value **Offset X, Offset Y and Angle** fields. These move or rotate the complete explosion after projection. They are separate from the sampled shape offset. The expandable construction diagram shows the generated deltoids before polar projection; the main canvas shows the final SDF result.

New recipes store the shape in `explosion.deltoid`, including undo, redo, style copies, project saves and managed project mapping. Existing triangle recipes without that field retain their geometry and seeded layout; **Use deltoid** in Shape explicitly converts them to the new shape defaults. Opening an inspector does not convert a saved recipe. The older `out`, `in`, disable and flip fields remain readable for compatibility and do not affect a deltoid. Missing new fields mean Shape X/Y = 1×, morph = 0% and blur = 0%, preserving existing appearances and fingerprints. Existing layer enum values and worker operations are unchanged. Preview and export use the same compositor. Without blur, inverse polar sampling avoids an intermediate strip image. Bounded angular buckets reduce candidate tests, render work is charged to the shared budget, and rows check cancellation. Existing progressive background previews remain in use.

Open **Explosion Explorer** from Tools or the layer's presets menu. Save, duplicate, rename, remove/undo removal, search and edit named presets with live previews. **Export collection** and **Import** use portable `.achexplosions` files. Each preset embeds its own shape, complete gradient stops/alpha, variation ranges and seed behavior, polar fit, transforms, opacity, blend and layer effects. Colors are independent copies, with no gradient-library dependency. Applying a preset from the matching layer's presets menu remains undoable and preserves the target's identity, name, folder and mask links.
